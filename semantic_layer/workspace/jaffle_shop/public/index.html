Jaffle Shop — where the margin actually is

Revenue is easy to see; profit is not. Every order item carries a supply cost, and tax is collected on the customer's behalf. Gross margin here is revenue minus supply cost, on two clearly separated bases: order-level revenue includes tax, item-level revenue is pre-tax. No number on this page mixes the two.
